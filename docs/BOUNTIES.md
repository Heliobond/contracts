# Contributor Bounties

This repository has **no verifiable record of contributor bounty payments at this time**. No transaction hashes, payout links, or paid-out bounty issues have been published by the maintainers.

## For prospective contributors

Before investing time in bounty work:

1. Ask the maintainers directly (comment on the bounty issue) for links to past payouts — e.g. on-chain transaction hashes or closed issues/PRs referencing payment.
2. Treat unverifiable bounty promises with appropriate skepticism and decide how much unpaid work you are comfortable risking.
